## 1. What you're getting

A copy of the BSF Solar Dispatch software bundle: the Node-RED dispatcher flow, the
dashboard, the Python relay/publisher, the Android widget, and the install tooling and
docs ("the Software"). You get to use it, modify it for your own installation, and run
it on as many of **your own** devices/sites as you like.

## 2. The honour-system trial

The dashboard shows a donation reminder 30 days after you first open it. This is a
**reminder, not a lock on the dispatcher** — your hot water, air-con and the rest keep
running whether or not the reminder is showing. Entering a valid licence key (a UUID
from your Gumroad receipt) dismisses the reminder permanently. The trial and key are
stored in your own browser; there is no server checking up on you. It works on trust,
because the people who'd bother to defeat it were never going to pay anyway.

## 3. Paying

listing. Payment is a one-time thing. There is no recurring charge, no account, and no
phone-home. Suggested amounts and the link are in `meta/pricing.md` / the Gumroad page.
